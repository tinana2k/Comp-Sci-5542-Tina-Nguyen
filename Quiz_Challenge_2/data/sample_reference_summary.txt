Foundation models such as GPT-4, Llama 3, and Claude 3 are pretrained on trillions of tokens and
fine-tuned with RLHF to align model behavior, yet hallucination and sycophancy remain unsolved
challenges. Retrieval-Augmented Generation addresses the knowledge-grounding problem by retrieving
relevant passages from an external corpus at inference time and conditioning the LLM on verified
evidence, significantly reducing hallucination rates without requiring model retraining. Students
must clone the lab repository, read the Lewis et al. RAG paper, and submit a team project proposal
by Friday. Evaluating RAG systems requires multiple metrics spanning retrieval quality — Recall at
K, MRR, NDCG — and generation quality — ROUGE, BERTScore, and RAGAS — since optimizing for any
single metric risks sacrificing others.
